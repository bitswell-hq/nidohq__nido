# Measurements

Component figures for the circuits in this directory. The recovery circuit
that composes them (spec §6) is not built yet; its figures will be measured
when it is.

**How they are produced.** `scripts/measure.sh` installs and checks Perch's
pinned toolchain, compiles every package, and runs `scripts/measure.py`,
which rewrites the block below:

- gates: `bb gates -s ultra_honk` on each bench package's compiled ACIR.
  "Padded size" is the power of two the prover rounds the circuit up to,
  which sets proving time and the CRS a prover downloads;
- proving: `nargo execute` (witness generation) and
  `bb prove --scheme ultra_honk --oracle_hash keccak --zk`, the
  zero-knowledge flavor Perch's verifier accepts, on the inputs in each
  package's `Prover.toml`, which the test-vector generator writes (the
  Gmail-shaped header and its body where a package takes them). Every proof
  is checked
  with `bb verify --zk`. Times are medians of wall-clock time; memory is the
  largest peak resident set size of `bb prove`.

**The machine is shared and was heavily loaded** during the run (its load
average is recorded in the block). Proving times on it are upper bounds for
an idle machine of the same kind, not reference figures; gate counts do not
depend on load. Phone-class and browser figures are not measured here.

## Reading the figures

- `header_parse_1024` is the cost of every header check without SHA-256 or
  RSA. Its rows above (`header_scan_1024`, `header_from_1024`,
  `header_tags_1024`) are cumulative, so the difference between consecutive
  rows is the cost of the From parser, the DKIM-Signature tag checks and the
  Subject parser.
- `dkim_header_1024` is everything a header-only DKIM proof needs at
  1024-byte headers: the checks of `header_sha_1024`, `rsa_2048` and
  `header_parse_1024` plus the key hash and output packing. Its gate count
  is below the sum of those rows because lookup and range tables are built
  once per circuit. The P2 hashes the recovery circuit adds on top
  (commitment, Subject opening, registry leaf and Merkle path) are
  `spec_hashes`, so the recovery circuit is expected to stay at 2^18
  [estimate, to be measured when it is built].
- Every proof is 16,224 bytes, the size of the zero-knowledge UltraHonk
  proofs Perch's adapter accepts, whatever the circuit size.
- The body hash grows by one SHA-256 compression per 64 bytes of bound;
  the recovery proof does not use it (spec §4.4).
