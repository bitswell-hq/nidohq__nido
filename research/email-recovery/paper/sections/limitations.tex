\section{Limitations and open questions}\label{sec:limits}

\begin{itemize}
  \item All four properties of \S\ref{sec:properties} are unproven, and the
    assumptions in \S\ref{sec:threat} are stated, not established.
  \item The provider learns that its user sent a recovery email, and when.
  \item Proving in a phone browser is slow; a progress indicator covers the
    wait, and its duration is to be measured.
  \item A provider key rotation can stall a recovery for up to the registry
    timelock, unless the account pinned the new key.
  \item Custom domains and organisation tenants are not supported.
  \item The design depends on the allowlisted providers authenticating
    senders and signing the \texttt{From} and \texttt{Subject} headers.
\end{itemize}
