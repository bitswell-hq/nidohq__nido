\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Email can be a recovery factor for a smart account without anyone storing
the address: a salted commitment on chain, a DKIM proof generated in the
user's browser, and a per-account guardian that counts the proof as one
approval in an existing delayed recovery. Its four properties are proven in
Lean about a model of the guardian, the circuit's relation and Perch's
controller, under the stated assumptions; the measurements are pending.
