\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Control of an inbox can be one recovery factor for a smart account while the
chain holds only a salted commitment: a DKIM proof made in the browser over
an email whose Subject blinds the recovery statement, counted by a
per-account guardian as one approval in Perch's delayed recovery. Soundness,
replay resistance and one-factor safety are proven over a model, and privacy
in an ideal model, under stated assumptions; the parsing and the model's
agreement with the code are tested. What remains is the production enclave,
phone measurements, real provider mail, people's behaviour under phishing and
alerts, and an external audit before mainnet.
