\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Control of an inbox can be one recovery factor for a smart account without
anyone holding the address: a salted commitment on chain, a DKIM proof made in
the browser over an email whose Subject blinds the recovery statement, and a
per-account guardian that counts it as one approval in Perch's delayed
recovery. Its four properties are proven over a model whose agreement with the
code is tested, under stated assumptions; an email vote fits within a third
of a Soroban transaction\claim{onchain-verification-cost}, and a desktop
browser proves in seconds\claim{browser-proving-cost}. What remains is the
production enclave, phone measurements, real provider mail, and an external
audit before mainnet.
