# On-chain cost of an email approval

The resources of one `submit_guardian` carrying an email approval with a
real proof of the recovery circuit, for each launch domain's committed
fixture, on Nido's integration-test stack: Perch's controller and account
from their deployed wasm, the email guardian, `nido-email-verifier` and the
DKIM key registry from their wasm (`docs/email-recovery/spec.md` §8.6, §12).

Two readings of the same call:

- **Test host**: `cost_estimate()` after the invocation. The SDK documents
  that it does not model some production work (for example certain XDR
  round trips), and its fee is priced with the SDK's 2026-07-10 fee
  snapshot (the schedule soroban-sdk 27.0.6 hardcodes) without the
  transaction-size components.
- **Transaction**: the call re-executed in a fresh host over a snapshot of
  the ledger with the signed authorization entry enforced (the host's
  `invoke_host_function_in_recording_mode` in enforcing-auth mode), so the
  guardian's `__check_auth` and the proof verification run; the footprint
  and resources it records, the signed envelope built from them, and the
  resource fee `compute_transaction_resource_fee` gives under the same
  snapshot, transaction size included. Simulation that records authorization
  instead of enforcing given entries does not run custom accounts'
  `__check_auth`, so it would leave out the guardian and the verification.

Rent is left out of both fees. Neither is a network measurement, and the
snapshot is not today's mainnet schedule: mainnet's archived settings price
the same transaction at less than half (`README.md`, "On-chain cost of an
email vote", which also gives the networks' fees).
